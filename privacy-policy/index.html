Privacy Policy

App: Diary
Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Anirudha Pradhan ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use Diary.

In short: The App is designed to work offline. Your diary entries are stored locally on your device and are not sent to our servers.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account or login. We do not collect or receive your diary entries, personal notes, or mood records on our servers.

Information you enter, such as diary entries, titles, dates, moods, and tags, is stored locally on your device to provide the App's features.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Information stored in the App is used only to provide its diary functionality, including:

  • Creating, editing, and viewing diary entries.
  • Organizing and searching saved entries.
  • Displaying mood records and calendar history.

We do not use your diary entries for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your diary information is stored in the App's local storage on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your diary entries.

We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock and keeping its operating system up to date.

Local storage does not guarantee absolute security. Anyone who gains access to your unlocked device or its accessible data may be able to view your diary.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, share, or disclose your diary entries or personal information to third parties.

The App does not include advertising or analytics services that collect personal information.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your diary entries remain stored on your device until you delete them or remove the App and its associated data.

  • You can delete individual diary entries using the App's delete functionality.
  • Uninstalling the App generally removes its locally stored data.

Data included in Android backups, device transfers, or other backup services may be retained separately under the applicable backup service's policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect children's personal information on our servers.

If the App is intended for children, additional privacy requirements and safeguards may apply.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's functionality or privacy practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
